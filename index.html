Calculation Details

The estimate processes daily account activity using 30-day months and a 365-day year for daily account interest.

  • Daily account interest is calculated from the current outstanding account balance.
  • Deposits reduce the account balance on their scheduled simulated days.
  • Monthly expenses and the scheduled mortgage payment are modeled as account withdrawals on their selected days.
  • Mortgage interest is calculated monthly from mortgage principal only; it is not added to mortgage principal.
  • New account advances require the prior account balance to be at or below $1 and at least 30 days must pass between advances.
  • The plan succeeds only when both the mortgage and account balances reach $0.
